The Center: The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgender Community Center

The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual & Transgender Community Center (The Center) was established in 1983 at the height of the AIDS crisis to provide a safe and affirming place for LGBTQ+ New Yorkers to respond to the urgent threats facing the community.

Services:

  • Health Services: Including HIV, and substance recovery programs
  • Youth Drop-in center: Come in and build community with others
  • Community Case Work: The Center's case managers are ready to advocate for you

The Brooklyn Community Pride Center

Founded in 2008 to provide LGBTQ+ Brooklynites with programming and resources in their home borough, BCPC has grown from a small office at Brooklyn Borough Hall into the primary provider of LGBTQ+ community space, programming, and resources for NYC's most populous borough. In doing so, we are building a stronger, safer Brooklyn for everyone.​​​​

Services:

  • Mental Health: culturally-competent, confidential individual psychotherapy for LGBTQ+ individuals
  • Arts & Culture: arts and culture activities build community and connection around creative pursuits and passions
  • Immigrant Services: Services that specifically center the distinct needs and priorities of immigrant Brooklynites
Visit The BCPC's Website

Destination Tomorrow

Destination Tomorrow is a grassroots agency and the LGBTQ+ center of the Bronx borough. It is our belief that no individual should have to leave his, her, or their neighborhood to access LGBTQ+ specific services.

Services:

  • Housing: provides housing services for both migrant and transgender and gender nonconforming individuals.
  • Educational Enrichment: provides warp around educational services.
  • Prevention & Outreach: activities give the opportunity to spread the word that the Bronx has an LGBTQ+ Center
