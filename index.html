The Center: The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgender Community Center

The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual & Transgender Community Center (The Center) was established in 1983 at the height of the AIDS crisis to provide a safe and affirming place for LGBTQ+ New Yorkers to respond to the urgent threats facing the community.

Services:

  • Health Services: Including HIV, and substance recovery programs
  • Youth Drop in center: Come in and build community with other youths
  • Community Case Work: The centers case managers are ready to advocate for you
