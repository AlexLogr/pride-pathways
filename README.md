# pride-pathways
This is meant to be a a web and mobile directory to assit LGBTQI+ individuals to services in NYC and surrounding areas. NYC while being generally considered a city with high acceptance there are very little services or access to services that LGBTQI+ individuals can access. These 15-14% of homeless youth <a href="https://sw4youth.commons.gc.cuny.edu/statistics/">(CUNY)</a>, and 17% of homeless adults <a href="https://williamsinstitute.law.ucla.edu/publications/lgbt-homelessness-us/">(Williams Institute)</a> identify as LGBTQ. Homelessness already has many barriers to accessing services adding the layer of queer identity and safety of accessing services, tools such as these made for the community by the community have the oppertunity to build trust and expand access to services.
